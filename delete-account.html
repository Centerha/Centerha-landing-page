    What happens to your data

    Deletion erases your sign-in credentials, profile and avatar, device tokens, notifications, favourites, and reviews. Shared teams, fixtures and completed competitions can retain attribution to an erased internal account.

    Required booking, payment, refund, payout and audit evidence may retain identifying details with restricted access. Backups and operational logs expire separately under retention policies.

    Administrator account offboarding

    For a Centerha administrator account, contact a Centerha Super Admin through your internal channel to verify your identity and request offboarding.

    Request an offboarding review through Support. Choose Privacy and deletion and use your work email. Keep passwords, verification codes and identity documents out of the form.
